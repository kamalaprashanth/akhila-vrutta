Akhila Vrutta: privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026. Applies to the website kp-akhila-vrutta.web.app and the Android app (in.kp.akhilavrutta).

What the app is

A private app for one household's money, home and documents. Only accounts the owner has allowed can use it.

Your data is encrypted before it leaves your device

Everything you enter (transactions, accounts, household records) is encrypted on your device with a key made from your passphrase. Google Cloud (Firebase), where it is stored, holds only the encrypted form. Nobody else, including the developer, Google or Anthropic, can read it without your passphrase.

Files

Bills, warranty cards and photos you add are encrypted on your device and kept in a folder named "Akhila Vrutta" in your own Google Drive. The app asks Google Drive only for access to the files it created itself; it cannot see anything else in your Drive.

Sign-in

You sign in with your Google account through Google. The app uses it only to recognise you. Your email address is not shown in the app or shared.

The Advisor

If you ask the Advisor a question, that question and the figures it needs are sent to Anthropic's Claude through the app's own server to write the answer. They are not used to train models and are not kept by the app.

On your phone

Fingerprint unlock uses a key kept in your phone's secure hardware; your fingerprint never leaves the phone. Bill and maintenance reminders are scheduled on the phone itself and name the bill only, never an amount. The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a document.

What isn't done

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.
  • No PAN, full account numbers, PRAN or folio numbers are kept (last 4 digits only).

Deleting your data

Records you delete are removed from the cloud. To delete everything, contact the owner of the app, who can remove the account's encrypted data and the app's folder in Google Drive.
